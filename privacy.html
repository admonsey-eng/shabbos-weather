Privacy Policy – Shabbos Weather Messages

Bobov Monsey (Monsey, NY) operates the "Shabbos Weather Messages" SMS program. Effective date: October 22, 2026.

What we collect

We collect only your mobile phone number, your name (optional), and the date you gave consent. You provide this information directly to the synagogue office when you ask to join the program.

How we use it

Your number is used solely to send approximately one automated text message per week, on Friday afternoons, with a weather forecast for that evening. We do not use it for marketing or any other purpose.

No sharing with third parties

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We use Twilio only as the technical carrier to deliver the messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact the office to be removed.

Message frequency and cost

Approximately 1 message per week. Message and data rates may apply.
