Terms of Service – Shabbos Weather Messages

Program: "Shabbos Weather Messages" from Bobov Monsey sends members of the congregation a weekly SMS weather forecast for Friday evening, so they know whether to bring a raincoat or dress for snow.

How to join: Members join by contacting the synagogue office in person, by phone, or by email and asking to be added. The office administrator confirms the number and the member's agreement before adding it to the list. There is no automatic sign-up.

Message frequency: Approximately 1 message per week (Friday afternoons). Occasionally none, if no service is held.

Cost: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier.

Cancel: Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive one confirmation message and nothing further.

Help: Reply HELP or email admonsey@gmail.com.

Carriers: Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy: See our Privacy Policy. Numbers are never shared with third parties.
