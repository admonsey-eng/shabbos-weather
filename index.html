Shabbos Weather Messages – Bobov Monsey

A free weekly text message from the Bobov Monsey synagogue office, sent every Friday afternoon, telling you whether to expect rain or snow that evening so you know whether to bring a raincoat to Shul.

How to join

To be added to the list, contact the synagogue office and ask to receive the Shabbos Weather Messages:

  • Phone: (845) 213-7738
  • Email: admonsey@gmail.com
  • In person: at the Bobov Monsey synagogue office

The office will confirm your mobile number and your agreement before adding you. There is no online sign-up form.

What to expect

Approximately 1 message per week, Friday afternoons. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for help.
